# SERVENTICA — Partner Onboarding, Job Lifecycle & Payout Flow

```text
1. ONBOARDING & KYC
   - Partner downloads partner app, verifies phone via OTP
   - Submits KYC documents (Aadhaar/PAN, trade certificate, bank account)
   - Admin reviews, verifies, and approves partner profile

2. AVAILABILITY & JOB DISPATCH
   - Partner toggles status to AVAILABLE in assigned service zones
   - Dispatch engine routes eligible booking with estimated earnings
   - Partner accepts within SLA window

3. EXECUTION & TRAVEL
   - Turn-by-turn navigation to customer address
   - Arrival confirmed within geo-fence
   - Customer provides 4-digit Start OTP to begin job
   - Tasks executed according to standard operating procedure

4. SETTLEMENT & PAYOUTS
   - Job marked SERVICE_COMPLETED
   - Net earnings (Gross - Platform Commission + Tips) credited to partner wallet
   - Weekly/daily automated payout to verified bank account via payment gateway
```
