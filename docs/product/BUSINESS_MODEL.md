# SERVENTICA — Business Model & Marketplace Mechanics

## 1. Monetization Streams
1. **Commission on Service Execution**: Dynamic category-based commission (10% - 20%) deducted from partner gross payout.
2. **Customer Convenience / Platform Fee**: Flat ₹15 - ₹29 per order covering payment processing and insurance.
3. **Serventica Originals**: Premium verified packages with higher margin.
4. **Cancellation Policy & Surge**: Surge fee during peak demand hours distributed between partner incentives and platform operations.

## 2. On-Demand & Workforce Mechanics
- **Model**: Generalized workforce engine supporting Hourly, Daily, and Recurring models for Househelp, Gardeners, Electricians, Plumbers, and Appliance Technicians.
