# SERVENTICA — Service Catalog Taxonomy & Hierarchy

## 1. Catalog Hierarchy
```text
CATEGORY (e.g. Appliance Repair)
   └── SUBCATEGORY (e.g. AC Service & Repair)
         └── SERVICE (e.g. AC Deep Cleaning)
               ├── VARIANTS (e.g. 1 Ton, 1.5 Ton, 2 Ton)
               └── ADD-ONS (e.g. Anti-rust spray, Gas top-up)
```

## 2. Dynamic Pricing & Fulfillment Models
- **Fixed-Price**: Standard home cleaning, basic repair inspection.
- **Variant-Based**: AC capacity, refrigerator double-door vs single-door.
- **Add-On Based**: Consumable parts and supplementary service modules.
- **Quantity-Based**: Ceiling fan installation × N.
- **Inspection / Quote**: Custom plumbing overhaul, whole-house painting.
- **Duration / Hourly**: On-demand helpers, gardener, general househelp.
